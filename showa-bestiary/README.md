# SHOWA BESTIARY™
### The Great Japanese Bestiary — A Collectible Fictional Archive Universe

> *"I discovered a lost government monster archive from 1974."*

SHOWA BESTIARY is not a poster shop. It is a **premium collectible art IP** built
around a fictional conceit: in 1974 (Shōwa 49), a secret Japanese government agency
catalogued every legendary beast, apex predator, spirit, and yōkai in the islands and
beyond. The agency was dissolved. The classified archive survived. We are "releasing"
those archive pages as collectible artifacts.

Every product — print, card, sticker, artbook, collector box — is a *recovered document*
from that archive. The brand voice, design system, and product ladder all serve one
feeling: **discovery of a forbidden, beautiful, dangerous record.**

## The Universe at a Glance

- **3 Collections × 10 creatures = 30 launch cards** (room to grow to 100+).
- **Collection 01 — BEASTS** (`動物大図鑑` / *The Great Bestiary of Earth*): real apex predators, rendered as state-classified threats.
- **Collection 02 — SPIRITS OF JAPAN** (`日本の神獣図鑑`): native Japanese fauna elevated to divine/spirit status.
- **Collection 03 — YOKAI** (`妖怪大図鑑`): mythological monsters and folklore spirits — the most dangerous, most collectible tier.

Each creature is a *record*: ID number, kanji name, classification, habitat, threat level,
and three attribute icons — like Pokémon crossed with a 1970s natural-history field guide.

## How to Use This Repo

1. **Designers / illustrators** → start with `brand/01`, `04`, `05`, `06` and the `templates/` wireframes.
2. **Store / ops** → start with `catalog/creatures.json`, `catalog/SCHEMA.md`, `brand/08`.
3. **Founders / strategy** → start with `brand/09` and `brand/10`.

Everything here is a living spec. Treat the `catalog/creatures.json` file as the single
source of truth for IDs, kanji, and attributes; all products (cards, store, artbook) should
read from it so the universe stays internally consistent.
