# 09 — Kickstarter Strategy

**Goal:** fund the flagship *THE GREAT JAPANESE BESTIARY* artbook + collector box and
convert an audience into a community.
**Funding target:** €20,000–50,000 (set the public goal **low/achievable**, ~€12–15k, and
design the campaign to over-fund — funded-fast is the algorithm's best friend).

---

## 1. Why Kickstarter (not just Shopify)

- The artbook + collector box are **high-cost, batch-produced** — crowdfunding de-risks the print run.
- KS provides a **launch moment**, social proof, and a built-in audience of art-book/TCG backers.
- It cements the brand as a *real IP project*, not a print drop.

---

## 2. Pre-Launch (the campaign is won here)

| Phase | Timing | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Build | T-12 to T-8 wks | Finalize artbook sample spreads, box mockups, hero video |
| Audience | T-12 wks → launch | Drive to a **"Notify me" landing page**; goal 1,000–3,000 emails before launch |
| Content | ongoing | Behind-the-scenes archive lore on IG/TikTok/Reddit/ArtStation |
| Warm-up | T-1 wk | Email sequence: "the archive opens [date]"; reveal top reward |
| Day-1 plan | launch | Mobilize list in first 24–48h to hit funding fast |

**KPI:** ≥30% of funding goal in the first 48 hours (algorithm + "funded" badge → trust).

| At | Unlock |

Stretch goals reward *backers* (not just the company) — this drives sharing.

---

## 5. The Campaign Page (structure)

1. **Hero video (60–90s):** cinematic "you found the archive" narrative + product flythrough.
2. **The hook:** the 1974 Bureau story (lore sells the premium positioning).
3. **The artbook:** sample spreads, page count, production specs.
4. **The collector box:** unboxing-style flatlay of everything inside.
5. **The cards:** the collectible system + rarity.
6. **Reward tiers** with clear comparison table.
7. **Stretch goals** roadmap.
8. **Who/why:** the creators, the bigger IP vision (3-year roadmap teaser).
9. **Risks & fulfillment:** honest timeline, POD/print partners, shipping (EU first).
10. **FAQ.**

---

## 6. Budget & Margin Sanity (illustrative)

| Cost | Note |
|------|------|
| Artbook print (batch) | Largest line — quote 3 printers, MOQ-aware |
| Cards + box manufacturing | Held stock; quote at expected volume |
| Fulfillment & shipping | EU domestic first; surcharge non-EU honestly |
| KS + payment fees | ~8–10% off the top |
| Buffer | +15–20% for overruns/reprints |

Price tiers so the **blended margin survives KS fees + fulfillment**; never price the box at
break-even. Validate unit economics *before* setting the public goal.

---

## 7. Launch & Live-Campaign Playbook

- **30-day campaign** (sweet spot; momentum without fatigue).
- Daily updates first week, then 2–3×/week: stretch progress, lore drops, backer spotlights.
- Cross-promote with other art-book / TCG / kaiju creators.
- Light paid retargeting to the warm "Notify me" list mid-campaign.
- **Final 72h push:** "archive closes" urgency to the full list + lapsed visitors.

---

## 8. Post-Campaign → Pledge Manager → Shopify

- Use a pledge manager (BackerKit/Crowd Ox) for add-ons & address collection (extra revenue).
- Fulfill on the promised timeline; over-communicate delays.
- **Convert backers to Shopify customers**: late pledges + ongoing creature drops keep the
  store alive after KS. The campaign is the *ignition*, not the destination.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

- [ ] 1,000+ pre-launch emails
- [ ] Funded within 48h
- [ ] ≥ €20k raised (target €20–50k)
- [ ] ≥ 300 physical-tier backers (future Shopify base)
- [ ] Fulfilled on schedule, < 3% defect/refund
- [ ] Email list + community carried into Phase 5 (licensing)
