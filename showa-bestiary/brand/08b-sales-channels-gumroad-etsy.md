# 08b — Sales Channels: Gumroad + Etsy + Shopify

> You have a **Gumroad** account, an **Etsy** account, and you want **Shopify** too. Good —
> run all three as **one system**, each doing what it's best at. This doc divides the roles
> so they reinforce (not cannibalize) each other. The full Shopify build-out lives in
> [08-shopify-store-concept.md](08-shopify-store-concept.md); this doc shows how it fits
> alongside Gumroad and Etsy.
>
> **TL;DR three-channel split:** **Etsy** = discovery/marketplace traffic ·
> **Gumroad** = digital + owned audience/memberships · **Shopify** = the branded "Archive
> Terminal" home and premium/collector hub. You can launch Etsy + Gumroad in week 1 and
> stand up Shopify in parallel (or right after the first sales) — they all read from the same
> `creatures.json` and brand system, so adding a channel is listing work, not a rebuild.

---

## 1. Channel Roles — Who Sells What

Use each platform for what it's actually good at. Don't duplicate blindly.

| | **Etsy** | **Gumroad** | **Shopify** |
|--|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Role | Discovery / marketplace | Digital + owned audience | Branded home / premium hub |
| Best for | **Physical** prints, cards, gifts | **Digital** files, PWYW, memberships | Full catalog, bundles, collector tier, artbook & box |
| Audience | Built-in search buyers ("yokai poster") | People you bring (IG/TikTok/email) | Your brand traffic + repeat collectors |
| Fees | Listing + transaction + payment + ads | Low flat % — you keep the relationship | Monthly plan + low txn fee, but you own everything |
| Strength | SEO/search traffic, trust, gift buyers | Instant delivery, memberships, list-building | Full control of UX, lore, scarcity, data, upsells |
| Weakness | Fees stack; you don't own the customer | No native discovery — you drive traffic | No built-in traffic; needs setup + marketing |

**Strategy:**
- **Etsy** — let the marketplace *find new buyers*; physical prints/cards + a few digital listings.
- **Gumroad** — high-margin *digital* + your *owned audience* (free PWYW sample → email list → memberships).
- **Shopify** — the *branded "Archive Terminal"* and **premium/collector hub** (numbered editions,
  artbook, collector box). This is where the universe lives; the other two feed it.

**Sequence (optional but recommended):** launch Etsy + Gumroad in week 1 for fast traffic and
cash flow, and stand up Shopify in parallel as the brand home. If bandwidth is tight, Shopify
can follow within a few weeks — nothing is lost, since all three share the same catalog data.

## 2. Gumroad Plan (digital-first, high margin)

Gumroad is ideal for the *archive* fiction because it can deliver "recovered files" instantly.

**Launch products:**
1. **Digital archive prints** — high-res printable PDF/PNG of each creature poster
   (A4 + A3 print-ready). Sell singles + collection bundles.
   - Single record: €4–7 · Collection (10): €25–35 · Full archive (30): €59–79.
2. **"Field Dispatch" digital pack** — wallpapers (phone + desktop) per collection. €5–9.
3. **Sticker/printable sheets** — print-at-home sticker sheets & postcards. €3–5.
4. **The Artbook PDF** (when ready) — digital edition of *The Great Japanese Bestiary*. €15–25.
5. **Pay-what-you-want "Sample File"** — one free/PWYW creature → top-of-funnel + email capture.
6. **Membership (Phase 2):** "Bureau Clearance" — subscribers get each newly *declassified*
   creature monthly. Recurring revenue + completionist hook.

**Gumroad setup checklist:**
- [ ] Profile = "the Bureau": agency seal avatar, dark banner, in-universe bio.
- [ ] Use the brand microcopy ("ACQUIRE RECORD", "REQUEST CLEARANCE").
- [ ] One product per creature + bundle products (Gumroad supports versions/bundles).
- [ ] Cover images = the archive page art with classification stamp.
- [ ] Turn on email capture / "follow" — this is your **owned audience** (gold for Kickstarter).
- [ ] Add a free PWYW sample to grow the list fast.
- [ ] Delivery: print-ready files + a short "handling instructions" PDF for the lore.

## 3. Etsy Plan (physical + discovery)

Etsy buyers are actively searching — lean into **SEO and physical product**.

**Launch products:**
1. **Physical art prints** — A4 / A3 / poster, per creature. Core seller.
2. **Print bundles / sets** — "Collection 01 Beasts — set of 3/5/10".
3. **Sticker packs & postcards** — cheap entry, great for reviews + add-ons.
4. **Trading card packs / sets** (when produced) — strong collector search demand.
5. **Digital download listings** — Etsy also sells digital; mirror a few Gumroad files here
   to catch Etsy's digital-art search traffic (keep pricing consistent).

**Fulfillment:** use an Etsy-integrated **print-on-demand** partner (Printful, Printify,
Gelato, Prodigi) so you hold no stock. *Hold stock only for trading cards / artbook / box.*

**Etsy SEO (this is the whole game on Etsy):**
- Titles: front-load real search terms + the brand fiction, e.g.
  `Japanese Tiger Art Print — Showa Bestiary Yokai Poster, Vintage Kaiju Wall Art (A3)`.
- 13 tags, all used: `japanese art print`, `yokai poster`, `kaiju art`, `vintage japanese`,
  `dark academia`, `ukiyo-e modern`, `monster art`, `showa`, `bestiary`, `wall art` …
- All 10 photo slots: framed mockup, in-room scene, detail/texture crop, the stamp/ID
  closeup, "collection" flatlay, size chart. Lifestyle shots convert.
- One short **video** per hero listing (slow pan over the texture/aging).
- Categories + attributes filled completely; offer A4/A3/poster as **variations**.

**Etsy setup checklist:**
- [ ] Shop name + banner + "About the Bureau" story section (Etsy rewards a strong story).
- [ ] POD partner connected + test order for color/quality.
- [ ] 10–15 strong listings at launch (one per bestselling-likely creature), then expand to 30.
- [ ] Shipping profiles (EU/DE first), processing times honest.
- [ ] Reviews funnel: include a small printed "archive card" thank-you asking for a review.
- [ ] Modest Etsy Ads budget on your best listings once you have a few sales/reviews.

## 4. Cross-Channel Funnel (how they work together)

```
        Social / lore content (IG · TikTok · Reddit · Pinterest)
                              │
        ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
        ▼                     ▼                      ▼
     ETSY                 GUMROAD                 SHOPIFY
  physical prints,     digital files, PWYW    branded "Archive Terminal":
  marketplace search   sample, membership     full catalog, bundles,
        │                     │               collector tier, artbook/box
        │                     │                      │
        └──────► EMAIL LIST / "Bureau Clearance" ◄────┘
                              │
                              ▼
              KICKSTARTER (artbook + collector box)
                              │
                              ▼
        fulfilled via Shopify (becomes the long-term home)
```

- **Pinterest + Etsy** are a strong pair for art prints (visual search → listing).
- Put your **Shopify/Gumroad link in Etsy** package inserts/thank-you (drive marketplace
  buyers to channels you own — "claim more declassified files at the Bureau archive").
- All three channels feed **one email list** — the asset that makes the Kickstarter work.
- **Shopify is the destination:** Etsy brings strangers, Gumroad converts them to fans,
  Shopify is where committed collectors complete the archive and buy the premium tiers.
- Avoid channel conflict: keep **collector/numbered/box exclusives on Shopify**, keep
  **discovery prints on Etsy**, keep **digital/membership on Gumroad**. Overlap only on the
  common entry prints, priced consistently (see §5).

## 5. Pricing Consistency (avoid undercutting yourself)

| Product | Gumroad (digital) | Etsy (physical) |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Single creature | €4–7 download | €15–25 print (A4/A3) |
| Collection (10) | €25–35 | €60–110 set |
| Stickers/postcards | €3–5 sheet | €6–12 pack |
| Artbook | €15–25 PDF | (physical via KS/Shopify) |

Keep digital cheaper than physical, and **never** make the same item cheaper on one platform
in a way that trains buyers to wait. Bundles should always beat buying singles.

---

## 6. How the Three Fit Together

- **Etsy** — discovery + physical prints/cards via POD. The marketplace finds new buyers.
- **Gumroad** — digital files, the free PWYW sample, bundles, and the "Bureau Clearance"
  membership. Your highest-margin, list-building channel.
- **Shopify** — the branded **Archive Terminal** (doc 08): the full 30-creature catalog,
  collection pages, lore, the Threat Index, and the **collector tier** (numbered editions,
  metal/canvas, artbook, collector box). This is the long-term brand home and Kickstarter
  fulfillment hub.
- **Same spine everywhere:** catalog data, SKUs, brand voice, and the stamp/lore system are
  identical across all three — everything reads from
  [`creatures.json`](../catalog/creatures.json). Adding a channel is listing work, not a rebuild.
- **Roadmap impact:** this means Phase 1 (doc 10) runs Etsy + Gumroad + Shopify together,
  rather than Etsy/Shopify alone — pull the Shopify milestone forward into Year-1 Q1–Q2.

## 7. First-Weeks Action List (with what you have)

2. [ ] **Gumroad:** set up Bureau profile + digital singles + 1 free PWYW sample + Beasts bundle.
3. [ ] **Etsy:** connect a POD partner; publish 5–10 physical print listings with full SEO + photos.
4. [ ] **Shopify:** spin up the dark editorial theme + 3 collection pages + first creature PDPs
   (full build-out in [08-shopify-store-concept.md](08-shopify-store-concept.md)); reserve
   numbered/collector items as Shopify-exclusive.
5. [ ] Add the footer warning + classification stamps to every cover/mockup (authenticity).
6. [ ] Start the email list (Gumroad follows + Shopify/Klaviyo form) — frame as "Request Clearance".
7. [ ] Post 3–5 lore/process pieces on IG/TikTok/Pinterest driving to all shops.
8. [ ] Insert a Shopify/Gumroad link + review request in every Etsy shipment.
9. [ ] Keep pricing consistent across channels (§5); make collector/numbered tiers Shopify-only.
