# 10 — Three-Year Roadmap

How SHOWA BESTIARY grows from a brand bible into a scalable collectible art IP. Phased to
match the business model: **Etsy/Shopify → Collector editions → Artbook → Kickstarter →
Licensing.** Each year compounds the last.

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **Universe first.** Every product deepens one coherent fiction. Consistency = IP value.
2. **Ladder up.** Move buyers from €5 stickers to €149 boxes to licensed goods.
3. **Scarcity as canon.** Classification tiers and numbered editions are lore *and* economics.
4. **Data-driven catalog.** `creatures.json` is the single source of truth across all products.
5. **Community = moat.** Collectors who want to *complete* the archive are the real asset.

---

## YEAR 1 — ESTABLISH (Phases 1–2)

**Theme: prove the universe and the unit economics.**

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---------|-----------|
| Q1 | Finalize brand system (this repo). Produce Collection 01 (Beasts, 10 archive pages). Launch Shopify + Etsy with prints, stickers, postcards (POD). |
| Q2 | Produce Collection 02 (Spirits). Launch trading cards (Beasts set). Start "field dispatches" email + social lore content. Begin numbered/limited prints. |
| Q3 | Produce Collection 03 (Yokai). Introduce collector tier: metal posters, canvas, numbered editions. Build pre-launch list for Kickstarter. |
| Q4 | Full 30-creature catalog live. "Newly declassified" drop cadence. Holiday bundles + collector box concept teased. Hit first revenue/list targets. |

**Year-1 goals:** all 30 creatures shipped as prints + cards; 3,000–10,000 email list;
validated bestselling creatures/formats; profitable POD base; KS audience primed.

---

## YEAR 2 — AMPLIFY (Phases 3–4)

**Theme: the flagship artbook + Kickstarter make it a real IP.**

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---------|-----------|
| Q1 | Produce *THE GREAT JAPANESE BESTIARY* artbook + collector box. Finalize lore bible. |
| Q2 | **Run Kickstarter** (€20–50k target). Fund artbook + box; unlock stretch goals. |
| Q3 | Fulfill KS via pledge manager. Convert backers → Shopify. Reprint/restock bestsellers. |
| Q4 | Launch **Collection 04** (new 10 creatures, e.g. "Sea/Deep" or "Sky/Storm"). Open card master-set + chase variants. First apparel test (1–2 designs). |

**Year-2 goals:** funded & fulfilled artbook; 40 creatures in canon; collector box as
flagship SKU; community of completionists; first non-print product line (apparel) validated.

---

## YEAR 3 — EXPAND & LICENSE (Phase 5)

**Theme: turn the IP into a platform and license it.**

| Quarter | Milestones |
|---------|-----------|
| Q1 | Launch **trading-card game layer** (simple battle/top-trumps) using existing stats. Subscription/"clearance club" for monthly drops. |
| Q2 | **Licensing deals:** apparel, skateboards, wall-art partners. First **gallery/pop-up exhibition** ("The Recovered Archive"). |
| Q3 | Collection 05 + special collabs (guest artists / regional yokai). International expansion (US/JP stores, localized). |
| Q4 | *Bestiary Vol. II* artbook (second Kickstarter, larger). Explore card-game distribution, exhibitions, and brand partnerships. |

**Year-3 goals:** 50+ creatures; multiple licensed product categories; recurring revenue
(subscription); exhibition presence; IP valued as a franchise, not a poster shop.

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Seen as "AI art posters" | Lead with lore, archive fiction, premium finish; never market as AI |
| Kanji/cultural errors | Native/fluent review before every print run |
| Inventory risk on artbook/box | Kickstarter to pre-fund; POD for everything possible |
| Catalog drift / inconsistency | `creatures.json` as single source of truth |
| Audience fatigue | Scheduled "declassified" drops + stretch-goal community involvement |
| Margin erosion (KS fees/shipping) | Validate unit economics before public goal; EU-first shipping |
| Cultural appropriation concerns | Respectful, well-researched treatment; consider JP collaborators by Year 3 |
