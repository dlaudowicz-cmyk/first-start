## 5. STORYBOARD — 5 CUTS

### CUT 1 — WIDE ESTABLISHING `4s`
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic | **Move:** CRANE-DOWN | **Framing:** WIDE → MEDIUM-WIDE
Max steht klein im goldenen Maisfeld, Wind streift die Stauden. Über ihm/hinter ihm pulsiert das Alien-Raumschiff, drückt einen perfekten Kreis ins Feld. Atmosphärisch, weit, klein-gegen-groß.

**Prompt (Smart Shot input):**
> Wide cinematic shot, 24mm anamorphic, slow crane-down from 8m to 2m eye-level. A young boy with dark messy hair, blue t-shirt and dark shorts stands small in a vast golden cornfield at dusk. Behind him, partially hidden by tall corn stalks, a metallic-organic alien spacecraft hovers low, emitting pulsing cyan-purple light that flattens the corn into a perfect circle around it. Twilight indigo sky, rising moon, last orange sunlight on western horizon. Ground mist between stalks. Pixar 3D CGI animation quality, photorealistic fur and skin shaders, volumetric lighting, shallow depth of field, 8K detail.

---

### CUT 2 — COCKPIT INTERIOR `3s`
**Lens:** 35mm | **Move:** MATCH-CUT from eye → INTERIOR | **Framing:** MEDIUM, three-shot
Wir cutten von Max' Auge nach innen — in das transparente Glas-Cockpit hinter seiner Stirn. Drei Mäuse-Piloten sitzen in Sesseln vor dem "Auge-Fenster". Holografische Anzeigen pulsieren rot. Warmer Amber-Glow.

**Prompt:**
> Interior shot inside a transparent glass dome cockpit located inside a boy's head, viewed from behind the pilot seats looking through the boy's eye-window onto the cornfield outside. Three small Pixar-style mice sit in ergonomic pilot chairs: left seat — a mouse with blue-grey fur and a wild blue mohawk hairstyle, amber eyes wide with panic; right seat — a cheerful grey mouse with spiky messy fur grinning with adventure-excitement; center-back seat — a curious grey mouse with golden eyes observing calmly. Warm amber interior lighting, holographic red alert displays pulsing on console surfaces, cyan reflections from the alien craft visible through the eye-window. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, Pixar 3D CGI, photorealistic fur simulation, 8K detail.

---

### CUT 3 — MAX REACTION `3s`
**Lens:** 50mm | **Move:** PUSH-IN | **Framing:** CLOSE-UP
Max' Gesicht: Augen werden riesig, Mund öffnet sich langsam. Cyan-Licht reflektiert in seinen Iris. Über seine Stirn schimmert *durchscheinend* das Cockpit der Mäuse mit. Magic moment.

**Prompt:**
> Close-up emotional push-in shot, 50mm lens, of a young boy's face. Dark messy hair, large brown-black expressive eyes widening with awe and fear, mouth slowly opening. Cyan alien light reflects in his iris and across his cheeks. Subtly visible through his forehead — like an almost-transparent overlay — three tiny mice in a glass cockpit can be glimpsed reacting inside his head. Twilight backlight, soft amber rim light. Cinematic Pixar 3D animation, photorealistic skin shader, shallow depth of field. Camera slowly pushes in from medium-close to extreme close-up.

---

### CUT 4 — ALIEN-KRIEGER REVEAL `5s`
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic | **Move:** LOW-ANGLE TILT-UP | **Framing:** LOW-WIDE → MEDIUM
Aus Max' POV (low angle, kniehoch). Schritt für Schritt: zuerst lila-gold Stiefel, die durch Bodennebel stampfen, dann Tilt-Up auf den massiven blauen Alien-Krieger. Cyan-Brustpanel leuchtet. Hörner. Finsterer Blick. Steam aus seinem Maul.

**Prompt:**
> Low-angle dramatic tilt-up shot, 35mm anamorphic. Starting at knee height, camera tilts upward to reveal a large imposing alien warrior emerging through low ground mist. Massive figure with thick blue fluffy fur, intricate purple-and-gold armor with a glowing cyan display panel on the chest, small horns and small feather-like ears, broad muscular shoulders, small feet visible at start of tilt. Grim threatening expression, small fangs visible, breath steam exhaling through nostrils. Twilight indigo sky behind, cornfield silhouettes flanking, cyan rim light from spacecraft behind illuminating armor edges. Pixar 3D CGI quality, photorealistic fur simulation with individual strand detail, metallic armor shader. Action continuity, slow menacing reveal.

---

### CUT 5 — COCKPIT PANIC `7s` → HARD CUT
**Lens:** 28mm wide | **Move:** DOLLY-IN + WHIP-PAN | **Framing:** WIDE COCKPIT → ECU red lever
Inside cockpit: Blue Mohawk Maus springt vom Sitz, hechtet zum roten Hebel ("ABBRUCH!"). Sammy reißt beide Pfoten hoch ("ABENTEUER!"). Maus (curious) hält ein Klemmbrett, kritzelt notes ("Faszinierend…"). Whip-pan zum roten Hebel. Pfote landet drauf. **HARD CUT** auf Schwarz.

**Prompt:**
> Wide dynamic shot inside the cockpit dome. Blue-mohawk mouse leaps from left pilot seat toward a glowing red emergency lever on the right console, mouth open shouting. Right-seat mouse — cheerful grey with spiky fur — throws both paws up in excitement, eyes huge with joy. Center mouse with golden eyes calmly writes notes on a tiny holographic clipboard. Warm amber light pulsing red as alarm strobes. Red console graphics, cyan reflections from outside. Camera dollies in fast, then whip-pans to extreme close-up of the red lever as a tiny paw slams down on it. Pixar 3D CGI, photorealistic fur, slight motion blur on quick action. Sound-cue: alarm klaxon. Cut to black on impact.

## 6. LIGHTING APPROACH

- **Primary motivator (exterior):** Twilight sky soft fill + alien craft cyan/purple keylight from BG-camera-left
- **Rim:** Last warm sun glow camera-right (low, 5° above horizon)
- **Cockpit interior:** Warm amber practicals (console glow) + red alert strobe pulses (8 BPM) + cyan rim from eye-window
- **Max close-up:** Cool cyan key on left cheek (alien), warm amber fill from right (sunset)
- **Krieger reveal:** Hard cyan backlight, low amber kicker on armor edges
- **Cut 5 cockpit:** Red alert flash dominates last 2s, amber pushed down 60%

## 8. CINEMATOGRAPHY NOTES

- Anamorphic flare allowance on cyan light sources (lens artifact = production-quality feel)
- Shallow DOF on all close-ups (T2.0 equivalent)
- Foreground corn stalks blur in Cut 1 and Cut 4 for layered depth
- Practical motion blur on whip-pan in Cut 5 (do NOT clean up)
- Color grade: ACES rec.709, slight teal-orange complementary push, deep blacks #0A0E1F
- Frame rate: 24fps native, no slowmo — comedy timing relies on cut rhythm

---

## 9. STYLE & TEXTURE NOTES

- Photorealistic fur simulation on ALL fur characters (Sammy, Blue Mohawk Maus, Maus curious, Alien-Krieger)
- Each mouse: ~120k individual fur strands minimum, with anisotropic specular for amber reflections
- Alien-Krieger armor: metallic shader, weathered brass + amethyst purple, micro-scratches
- Max skin: subsurface scattering on, freckles barely visible
- Corn stalks: ~40 unique base meshes, GPU-instanced, wind sim 12mph east
