# FLUFFLAND — Series Bible v1.0

### Core Vision, Character Canon, World Rules & Environment Guide

> **CONFIDENTIAL.** This document is the single source of truth for all FLUFFLAND
> development: animation, storyboards, AI generation, character design, background
> design, merchandise, licensing, publishing, pitch decks, marketing.
>
> **No character, environment or story element may contradict this document.**

---

## One-sentence premise

FLUFFLAND tells soft emotional adventures about beloved plush toys who come alive
each night in a dream world while children sleep.

Everything is warm. Everything is safe. Everything is made of love.

## Target audience

- **Primary:** children aged 2–6
- **Secondary:** parents and caregivers

## Emotional DNA

Every episode should feel like *"a tiny bedtime adventure with friends."*

- **Core:** belonging · comfort · patience · friendship · courage · kindness · curiosity · reassurance
- **Never:** fear · danger · punishment · competition · winning vs losing · sarcasm · cynicism

## Visual DNA

- **Must feel:** soft · plush · handcrafted · sewn · cuddly · tactile · warm
- **Never feel:** digital · futuristic · metallic · cold · realistic · industrial

## Universal character rules

All characters share: small black button eyes · eyes close together · matte eyes ·
rounded silhouettes · plush textures · soft edges · simplified forms ·
child-readable emotions · no realistic animal anatomy.

**Forbidden:** realistic eyes · visible teeth · sharp shapes · hard surfaces · aggressive expressions.

## Character canon

### Crossi
- **Species:** Croissant plush toy
- **Personality:** optimistic · warmhearted · slightly clumsy · comforting
- **Physical:** **NO legs · NO feet** · short plush arms · large croissant silhouette
- **Important:** Butter is almost always visible, usually sitting on Crossi
- **Never:** realistic croissant · human anatomy · legs · shoes
- **Function:** Main mascot · primary merchandising character

### Butter
- **Species:** Butter block plush
- **Personality:** adorable · emotional · expressive
- **Physical:** **NO legs · NO feet** · tiny arms
- **Communication:** squeaks · chirps · emotional sounds
- **Never:** speak words · walk · become independent for long periods
- **Function:** Non-verbal comedy

### Cora
- **Species:** Heart pillow plush
- **Personality:** empathetic · caring · emotionally intelligent
- **Physical:** heart-shaped body · visible patch/bandage · short plush arms
- **Function:** Comfort and emotional resolution

### Nana
- **Species:** Hippo plush
- **Personality:** protective · patient · loving · nurturing
- **Unique feature:** Nana has a **zipper integrated directly into her belly fur**.
  **NOT a pouch. NOT clothing. NOT an accessory.** The zipper opens into a soft
  interior space inside Nana's body. Clip & Clap travel, rest and hide inside.
- **Physical:** zipper always present · zipper integrated into body design · soft plush
- **Never:** backpack · pouch · kangaroo bag · baby carrier
- **Function:** Mother figure of Fluffland

### Clip & Clap
- **Species:** Baby hippo plush toys
- **Personality:** playful · curious · energetic
- **Rules:** usually travel inside Nana · can peek out of the zipper opening · visually related to Nana
- **Communication:** mostly sounds and reactions
- **Function:** Baby humor and family stories

### Risto
- **Species:** Monkey **hand puppet** — Risto is NOT a monkey, he is a monkey hand puppet
- **Physical:** **NO legs · NO lower body** · puppet body only · resembles classic puppet-theatre characters
- **Movement:** bouncing · popping · puppet-like movement · exaggerated gestures
- **Never:** walk normally · run · receive monkey anatomy
- **Function:** Comedy and energy

### Lunelle
- **Species:** Unicorn plush
- **Personality:** dreamy · imaginative · hopeful
- **Features:** crown · tutu · magical aesthetic
- **Function:** Dreams, wishes and fantasy

### Manny
- **Species:** Manatee plush
- **Personality:** calm · relaxed · thoughtful
- **Features:** Hawaiian shirt · soft rounded silhouette
- **Never:** stand like a human · move quickly
- **Function:** Slow living and mindfulness

### Zeddy
- **Species:** Old polar bear teddy
- **Personality:** wise · calm · dependable
- **Features:** visible patches · worn fabric · oldest-looking plush toy
- **Important:** largest character in Fluffland
- **Never:** aggressive · loud · young-looking
- **Function:** Mentor and emotional anchor

### Mini-Fluffs
- **Species:** Round plush fluff creatures
- **Physical:** **NO arms · NO legs** · round bodies
- **Representation:** girls · boys · non-binary
- **Differentiation:** color · hairstyle fluff · accessories
- **Never:** realistic anatomy · become major individual protagonists
- **Function:** Crowd reactions and visual comedy

## Golden character rule

Every character must be recognizable as a **black silhouette**. If the silhouette is
unclear, the design is wrong.

## World map

- **Fluff Gathering Place** — the heart of Fluffland; main gathering area; most stories begin here.
- **Fluff Café** — Crossi's favorite; warm drinks, star cookies, cloud cocoa.
- **Star Garden** — stars grow like flowers; home of wishes and dreams; Lunelle's favorite.
- **Cotton Candy Lake** — soft floating waters; Manny's domain.
- **Cuddle Forest** — plush trees, felt leaves, yarn vines; never scary.
- **Cloud Playground** — slides, swings, bouncy clouds; home of Clip, Clap and Mini-Fluffs.
- **Zeddy's Corner** — quiet resting area; storytelling; reflection; bedtime atmosphere.

## Environment style guide

- **Materials:** plush · fleece · wool · felt · cotton · bouclé
- **Secondary:** yarn · stitching · patches · ribbons · buttons
- **Forbidden:** steel · chrome · concrete · glass · machinery

## Color palette

- **Primary:** cream · cloud white · peach · lavender · pastel pink · baby blue · butter yellow
- **Accent:** mint · soft gold · coral
- **Forbidden:** neon colors · harsh black · aggressive contrast

## Sky rules

- **Preferred:** sunrise · sunset · magical twilight
- **Forbidden:** storms · threatening darkness · scary weather

## Plants

All plants are plush-inspired: star flowers · button flowers · yarn vines · cloud mushrooms. No realistic botany.

## Water

Water behaves softly: cotton candy water · foam streams · dream puddles · cloud ponds.

## Fluffland physics

Everything bounces gently · clouds support weight · nobody gets hurt · falling is harmless · floating is normal.

## Recurring world icons (must appear frequently)

⭐ fabric stars · ☁ cloud pillows · 🧵 stitching · 🩹 patches · 🧸 plush textures · 🎀 ribbons · 🧶 yarn

## Episode structure

1. Arrival
2. Small emotional need
3. Tiny obstacle
4. Friends help
5. Soft solution
6. Warm ending

Stories stay small. Feelings stay real. Solutions stay gentle.

## Final Fluffland rule

If a child wants to climb into the world, cuddle the characters and fall asleep there,
the design is correct. If the world feels cold, loud, stressful, realistic or dangerous,
the design is wrong.
